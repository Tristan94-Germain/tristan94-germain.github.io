2D Action Platformer · C++ / SFML

BioPulse

An underwater action platformer in pixel art. The player explores a submarine laboratory through a series of rooms full of enemies, all the way to the lair of the Giant Jellyfish boss.

  • Responsive movement: run, jump, dash, crouch, wall jump and ladders
  • Shooting system and a robot companion that turns into a platform
  • Three enemy types: crab, pufferfish and seahorse
  • Multi-phase boss fight (laser beam, falling tentacles)
  • Health & collectible shells, fixed HUD
  • Built-in tile-based level editor
  • Menus, options, pause with stats and gamepad support

Mobile Arcade · Unity · Game Jam

Kaboom Monsters

A mobile arcade game made in Unity during a game jam. Slide to aim the cannon and fire bombs to blow up the monsters, wave after wave, all the way to the boss.

My role: game flow and UI: power-ups, coins, screens and pause menu.

  • Waves of monsters ending with a boss fight
  • Three power-ups: Pierce, Burst and Power
  • Coins and a shop with power-ups and cannon skins
  • Rewarded ads: free power-ups and x2 coins on victory
  • Title screen, pause menu and victory screen
